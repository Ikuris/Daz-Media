How to Play

The case: the Order of the Broken Halo is ringing Vesperhollow's old bells toward a rite. One of five suspects leads them. Every dawn another bell tolls — at seven bells the rite completes and the final confrontation grows far deadlier.

Investigate: type anything — “examine the bell ropes”, “press Pike for names”, “read Madame Vale’s face while she lies”. The Game Master calls for d20 checks: roll + modifier vs a Difficulty Class.

Evidence & deduction: clues go to the Case board. Analyze each one to learn which suspect it points to. With three analyzed pieces against the same suspect you can Accuse — and end this.

Catch them lying: ask suspects for their alibi. If a piece of evidence contradicts it, present it to them in person. An innocent’s lie hides a private secret (that evidence stops counting against them); the culprit’s slip reveals their motive.

The Order fights back: from the fourth bell, the cult acts each dawn — burning crime scenes, frightening witnesses, buying informants and sending killers after you. Move fast.

Who, how, why: to accuse, you name the culprit, the method and the motive. A complete case weakens the final foe and unlocks the best ending.

Combat: attack, cast, use your discipline’s powers, drink tonics, dodge, flee — or snap the Enchanted Shackles on a wounded cultist and interrogate them for a lead.

AI Game Master: when available, Claude runs the city: suspects lie, witnesses remember you, and new places appear. The rules engine enforces dice, HP and evidence, so the story can’t cheat. Start a message with ? to ask the GM a question.

Music: 120 original loops that follow you through the city. Turn them on from the title screen or the ♫ button.
